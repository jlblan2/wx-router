    Saved routes (0)

      Check a saved route to run it against the latest forecast. Each check is remembered, so you can see whether conditions are getting better or worse.

      Vessel

      Routes stay out of water shallower than the minimum depth, using NOAA bathymetry. Those depths are measured from about high water, not chart datum, and small shoals can be missed, so leave a margin over your draft (draft plus 3 ft is a sensible start).

      Maximum seas allowed (ft)

      Set by where the seas strike the boat: eight points around the vessel, each covering 45°.

      Maximum winds allowed (kt)

      Sustained wind, set by where it comes from relative to the bow.

      Steep, short seas

      Avoid any area where the waves are higher than this and come this close together, whatever direction they come from.

      Cruising hours

      For example 18:00 until 08:00 keeps you off the water overnight. Times are this phone's local time. Every departure must arrive before the no-cruise hours start; on longer trips, add your own waypoints where you can anchor, and the route stops there overnight and goes on the next morning.

      Weather data

      The forecast source is kept separate from the planner so another service can be plugged in later.
